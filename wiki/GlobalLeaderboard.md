# Global Consensus Leaderboard
- **Logic:** Merges anonymized `open_data.json` exports from community participants.
- **Service:** `globalAggregator` (`.antigravity/global_aggregator.ts`) processes multi-source training data.
- **Export:** `/api/consensus/aggregate` generates the global ranking (skill, averageScore).
- **Goal:** Crowd-verified, open-source performance metrics across different breeds and skill sets.
